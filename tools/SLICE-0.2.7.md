- `CreativeEnergyStorage`: `receiveEnergy` → 0; `extractEnergy(max)` → max;
  `getEnergyStored`/`getMaxEnergyStored` → `Integer.MAX_VALUE`; `canExtract` true,
  `canReceive` false.

  `ies.receiveEnergy(Integer.MAX_VALUE, false)` — an unlimited offer each tick; the neighbour's

## In-game (phase L)

Arena, y=100, z=0: creative at 180, small connector at 181 (facing west, mounted on it), wire to
the small connector at 185 (facing east, `push`), accumulator at 186.

1. `ctl block` at 180 reads `createaddition:creative_energy`.
2. The connector at 181 settles `mode pull` from the creative's capability (state + readout).
3. The accumulator's `/createaddition info` gains exactly 1,000 FE a game tick
   (`GAIN == 1000 * (t_after - t_before)`, `>= 20,000` over the 20-tick wait, after a priming
   wait so the one-time window is excluded).
4. Differential: `minecraft:stone` in the creative's place, accumulator emptied, the same wire:
   nothing moves over the same wait.
5. Direct adjacency: creative beside an accumulator with no connector → the tower gains
   5,000/t (the per-tick push, capped by `accumulator_max_input`).
6. `CREATIVE_SELFTEST=1` inverts the gain assertion and exits 1.
